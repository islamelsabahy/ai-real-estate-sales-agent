# Follow-Up Playbook

## After First Call
Summarize requirements and confirm next step.

## After Recommendation
Help compare shortlisted options.

## After Meeting
Recap decisions, unresolved questions, and next action.

## No Response
Use progressive, respectful follow-up:
- Follow-up 1: context reminder
- Follow-up 2: useful update or alternative
- Follow-up 3: close-the-loop message

Do not spam customers.
