# Lead Qualification

## Example Score

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Budget Fit | 25 |
| Purchase Timeline | 20 |
| Requirement Clarity | 15 |
| Location Fit | 10 |
| Engagement | 10 |
| Financing / Payment Readiness | 10 |
| Decision-Maker Readiness | 10 |

Total = 100

## Example Classification

- 80–100: Hot
- 60–79: Warm
- 40–59: Nurture
- <40: Low Priority

These thresholds are examples and should be calibrated to actual business performance.

Lead score must never replace a salesperson's judgment or required company process.
